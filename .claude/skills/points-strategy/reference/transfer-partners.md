# Transfer partners (snapshot, verify before use)

Partner lists and ratios change several times a year, and partners get added and dropped.
**This file is a starting point for brainstorming, never the final word.** Before
recommending any transfer, confirm the partner and ratio on the bank's own current
partner page. When you find something here is out of date, fix this file and note the
date at the top.

Last reviewed: not yet verified (written from general knowledge, October 2026)

often at worse ratios; check the ratio before doing the math.

## Alliance cheat sheet (who can book whom)
- **Star Alliance**: United, Air Canada, Lufthansa, Swiss, Austrian, ANA, Singapore,
  Turkish, EVA, TAP, Avianca, and others. Book with United, Aeroplan, Avianca LifeMiles,
  Turkish, ANA, Singapore.
- **oneworld**: American, Alaska, British Airways, Iberia, Qatar, Cathay Pacific, JAL,
  Qantas, Finnair, and others. Book with AAdvantage, Alaska, Avios programs, Cathay,
  Qantas.
- **SkyTeam**: Delta, Air France, KLM, Korean Air, Virgin Atlantic, and others. Book with
  Delta, Flying Blue, Virgin Atlantic.
- Notable non-alliance partnerships: Virgin Atlantic books ANA and Delta; Etihad and
  Emirates have their own partner lists; Alaska has many partners outside oneworld.
