# Points valuations (starting benchmarks)

Rough, conservative cents-per-point (CPP) values for judging whether a redemption is
good. They're a snapshot, not a fact: refresh them from current published valuations
(The Points Guy, NerdWallet, Frequent Miler, One Mile at a Time) every few months, and
prefer the client's own numbers in `tina/points.md` when they've set them.

| Currency | Benchmark CPP | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Chase Ultimate Rewards | 1.5 - 2.0 | Value comes mostly from transfers (Hyatt especially) |
| Amex Membership Rewards | 1.5 - 2.0 | Strong airline partners; weak cash-out options |
| Capital One miles | 1.4 - 1.8 | Can also erase travel purchases at 1.0 |
| Citi ThankYou | 1.4 - 1.8 | Value depends on holding a premium card for full transfers |
| Bilt | 1.5 - 2.0 | Hyatt and strong airline partners |
| World of Hyatt | 1.5 - 2.0 | Usually the best hotel currency |
| Hilton Honors | 0.4 - 0.6 | Fifth night free on standard awards |
| Marriott Bonvoy | 0.6 - 0.9 | Dynamic pricing; fifth night free on awards |
| IHG One Rewards | 0.4 - 0.6 | Fourth night free with their co-brand card |
| United MileagePlus | 1.1 - 1.4 | Dynamic pricing on its own flights; partners are more stable |
| Delta SkyMiles | 1.0 - 1.2 | Fully dynamic; flash sales matter |
| American AAdvantage | 1.3 - 1.6 | Good partner awards (no or low surcharges on many) |
| Southwest Rapid Rewards | 1.2 - 1.4 | Tied to fare price; Companion Pass is the big lever |
| Alaska Mileage Plan / Atmos | 1.3 - 1.6 | Strong partner awards; stopovers on some |
| Air Canada Aeroplan | 1.3 - 1.6 | Distance chart for partners; stopover option |
| Flying Blue (Air France/KLM) | 1.2 - 1.5 | Monthly Promo Rewards discounts |
| British Airways / Iberia / Aer Lingus Avios | 1.2 - 1.5 | Distance-based; short-haul is the sweet spot |

Rules of thumb:
- Below 1.0 CPP on transferable points is almost always a bad use.
- Hotel currencies vary wildly. Judge Hilton and Marriott redemptions on their own scale.
- A redemption at 2x the benchmark or better is worth highlighting as a win.
