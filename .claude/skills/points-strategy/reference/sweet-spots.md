# Sweet spots and plays (historically strong, verify current pricing)

These have been reliably good value, but programs devalue without notice. Check the
current award chart or pricing before pitching one, and drop or update entries that no
longer hold.

## Hotels
- **Hyatt via Chase or Bilt.** Fixed-ish award chart with category pricing; top-tier
  properties often run far below their cash rates. Usually the best hotel use of
  transferable points.
- **Fifth night free** on Hilton and Marriott standard awards: price 5-night stays
  against 4.
- **Free night certificates** from co-brand cards: use them on the most expensive night
  (weekends in cities, peak resort nights) and check whether they can be topped up with
  points.
- **Points + cash** can unlock a great CPP when the client is short on points.

## Flights
- **Short-haul Avios.** Distance-based pricing makes short nonstops on American, Alaska,
  or Iberia cheap, especially when last-minute cash fares spike.
- **Flying Blue Promo Rewards.** Monthly discounted routes, often to Europe.
- **Aeroplan partner awards.** Distance-based pricing, generally no carrier surcharges
  on most partners, and a paid stopover on one-way awards.
- **Virgin Atlantic for ANA or Delta.** Virgin has historically priced ANA business and
  first and some Delta One routes attractively. Pricing has shifted before; verify.
- **Turkish Miles&Smiles** for some Star Alliance routes, including United-operated
  flights in the past. Watch for changes.
- **Alaska partner awards** (oneworld and others) with stopovers allowed on some.
- **Southwest Companion Pass.** One companion flies with the client for just taxes on
  every flight for the rest of the qualifying year and the next. Huge for couples who fly
  domestic often.
- **Positioning flights.** A cheap flight to a bigger hub can unlock much better award
  space or fares. Price it, but warn about separate-ticket risk.
- **Open-jaw and stopovers.** Fly into one city and out of another, or add a free or cheap
  stop, when the program allows.

## Timing plays
- Award calendars open at different times per airline (often around 330-360 days out).
  Premium cabin space is often best right when calendars open or close to departure.
- Transfer bonuses: if travel is far out and space is plentiful, it can pay to wait for
  one.
- Hotel and airline sales and promotions (bonus points per stay, award discounts).
