## Current plan (set 2026-11-10)
| Calories | Protein | Carbs | Fat |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1,800 | 145 g | 170 g | 60 g |

Why: 145 g ≈ 1.6 g/kg body weight, where most of the muscle-retention benefit plateaus.
Fat 60 g keeps a healthy floor for hormones. Carbs fill the rest and fuel training.
Expected loss ~1–1.5 lb/week after the first week's water drop.

Suggested daily shape (no latte): ready-to-drink shake (~30 g P, ~160 kcal) + lunch out
(~45 g P, ~650 kcal) + dinner at home (~60 g P, ~800 kcal) + one protein snack (~15–20 g P,
~140 kcal, e.g. Quest chips or 2 light string cheese) ≈ 1,750 kcal, ~150–155 g P.
Without the snack it's ~135 g, so the snack (or double protein at lunch) is what closes the gap.

## Phase plan
1. **Cut** (started 2026-11-10): ~8–12 weeks at 1,800 to about 182–185 lb.
2. **Build/recomp**: maintenance (~2,400–2,600 kcal), protein ~145–170 g, progressive
   lifting 3–4×/week. Weight holds steady while body composition improves.

## Adjustment rules agreed with the client
- Losing more than 2 lb/week after week 2, or lifts dropping 2–3 weeks in a row →
  add ~200 kcal, mostly carbs.
- Track the weekly average weight (several mornings a week) and waist at the belly
  button every two weeks.
